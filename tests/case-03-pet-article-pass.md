# case-03 · PET article 过线 Grade B（2026-09-29 校准）

## 输入

题目：PET Part 2 article，话题 FRIENDSHIP（同 case-01）。

作文（学生C，转写已确认，含标题 Friends and friendship）：

> I think it is better to have lots of friends. When you have many friends, more people can help you when you have problems. You also have more people to play and talk with, so you will seldom feel lonely.
> It is useful for friends to have similar charaters. If you think in similar ways, you understand each other easily and enjoy the same hobbies. But even if friends are different, you can still be good friends.
> In short, many friends bring more fun and support. Friendship makes our life happier.

词数 91，已过 90 词线。

## 老师确认过的期望结论

- 评分：C 4 · CA 4 · O 4 · L 4 = 16/20，Grade B 水平
- 均衡型画像：四项同分无短板，突破点在加例子（Content→5）和尝试高级词汇/复杂结构（Language→5）
