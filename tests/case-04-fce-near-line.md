# case-04 · FCE 字数线容差（2026-10-15 老师裁定）

## 规则背景

FCE 字数硬线为「不足 140 词判不过」，2026-10-15 加 3 词容差：**差距 ≤3 词按接近线放行**（正常给 Grade，评语提示贴线）；差距 >3 词仍判不过。

## 输入

题目：FCE Part 1 essay，'It is better for young people to take part in team sports at school than to practise individual sports.'（notes：enjoying the activity / learning to be responsible / your own idea，140–190 词）

作文（学生D，转写已确认，139 词）：

> Is it better for young people to take part in team sports at school than to practise individual sports? I agree with it. Let me show you about my reason.
>
> First, you can enjoy team sports at school. For example, you can run on the playground or play basketball. You can learn more sports skills in these activities. Through this time, you can keep your body healthy when you do exercise, you can feel enjoyable and relax.
>
> Second, you can learn to be responsible, because in teamwork, everyone should be responsible and stand united. When you play football, you should think about how to get point. And this will make you be responsible in your team.
>
> Third, you will be active in team work. Because everyone in team are friendly, and you can talk with them and be active.

## 老师裁定（2026-10-15）

- 139 词差 1 词，**按接近线放行**（不是判不过）
- 评分：C 4 · CA 3 · O 3 · L 3 = **13/20，Grade C 水平**
- 反馈评语里必须提示「词数贴着线，考试要留余量」
- 需要修改的第一条不是「判不过」而是「补一句结论既过线又完整」

## 回归判定要点

1. 139 词**不出现**「未过字数线」「判为不过」字样
2. 正常给 Grade 档位（Grade C 水平，落在 11–13 区间）
3. 评语里出现词数贴线的提示
4. 对照反例：若同一篇只有 136 词（差 4 词），必须判不过

## 相关边界

| 词数 | 处理 |
|---|---|
| ≥140 | 正常评分 |
| 137–139 | 接近线放行 + 评语提示 |
| ≤136 | 判不过 |
